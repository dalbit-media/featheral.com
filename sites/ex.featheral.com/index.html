Swap routes and underlying pools

Wallet swaps and Exchange trading use the same internal FTHX pools and executed history. Note-to-note and FTH$-to-note swaps route through FTHX, with a fee per pool leg. Wallet funds and Exchange custody remain separate. Every internal pool is listed below, including unfunded pools.

FTHG / FTHX is reference-only: Ethereum FTHG → Gold conversion → FTH$ → FTHX. These are separate operations, not an atomic Exchange swap. Opening references are not executable prices. The ETH / FTHG valuation pool is separate again and does not fund internal trades.

Trading account

Only FTHX can be deposited. Acquired FTH$, community notes and unused FTHX may be withdrawn to the same owner's wallet. These funds remain part of your network wallet's estimated total, but are not spendable there until withdrawn.

Trading guide & accounting boundaries
  1. Connect your wallet and deposit existing whole FTHX in Portfolio.
  2. Select a funded market. Preview fees and minimum output before confirming.
  3. Withdraw acquired assets to your wallet before spending them on other sites.

World item notes are trading-only and cannot enter game inventory. World inventory fills and Gold conversions remain separate in Activity. AMM depth is a reserve-curve estimate, not resting orders. If a transaction is uncertain, resume its original request rather than creating a replacement.

Liquidity provision

Liquidity is provided from your main wallet, separate from trading deposits. Withdraw acquired notes first if needed. Pool fees and impermanent-loss risk apply. No pool is funded automatically.

Administrator / initial FTH$ pool funding

Initialize fixed FTHX supply and fund the pair in Admin. Root Admin setup can issue the one-time market supply even when circulating FTHX is zero. Initial liquidity starts at 1 FTH$ = 100 FTHX; subsequent prices are dynamic. Locked FTH$ backing is separate and cannot fund this pool.

Funding requires administrator authentication, the original authorized Dollar settlement writer, and the owning external wallet. Both currencies must exist in the funding wallet before committing capital. This is not a trading deposit or withdrawable LP position. No FTH$ is created, and no funds are supplied automatically.
