Resources and residents

Wood is shown in kg, Water in L, Metal in tonnes and Oil in barrels. Assign residents to production, collect deliveries and maintain household Water supplies. Weather and seasons affect production and recruitment; the weather icon expands to explain the modifiers.

FTH$ and backing

Dollar owns the authoritative FTH$ ledger. Gold manages a finite reserve at 1 FTHG = 1,000 FTH$. World markets receive one-time funding, and players earn currency by selling resources—not by receiving personal grants. Gameplay payments circulate through markets and treasury.

Off-chain allocations do not move Ethereum tokens. Redemption requires confirmed backing. FTH$ is not a USD-redeemable asset.
