Made to connect

Your checkout.
Our payment request API.

Create a portable FTH$ request with JSON. Get a payment link, QR URL, NFC URL record and payer handoffs. No private keys, no new ledger.

POST /api/v1/pay/requests
Content-Type: application/json

{
  "to": "YOUR_PUBLIC_WALLET_ID",
  "amount": "12.50",
  "currency": "FTH$",
  "name": "Your business"
}

Omit amount for a reusable request. Requests are stateless and public, not invoices or proof of payment. Confirm receipt in your wallet or native ledger before providing goods.

GET /api/v1/pay/requests?to=...
GET /api/v1/pay/qr?to=... returns SVG.
