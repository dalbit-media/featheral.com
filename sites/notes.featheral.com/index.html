Onboard, mint & set up a pool
  1. Connect your existing Featheral wallet.
  2. Mint a community note and reserve its website. Existing creation fees apply; a new note is not automatically funded.
  3. Complete website setup, then review the note's pool. Initial funding requires administrator approval and existing assets.
  4. Once funded, manage liquidity and trade on Exchange. No supply or liquidity is created automatically.
