How conversion works

      
    
        
  1. Your wallet approves FTHG for the bridge contract on Ethereum Mainnet (gas applies).
    2. 
        
  2. The bridge's convertFthg call prices the trade on its constant-product curve with 1% slippage protection.
    3. 
        
  3. After the transaction confirms, the FTH$ writer seals a CREDIT for the same amount to your account.
    4. 
        
  4. Withdrawals back to FTHG require the authorized settlement service and appear here as RESERVE → SETTLE; this wallet does not submit redemption requests.
