FeatheralDatabase

File-ledger infrastructure

Per-token journals for FTHG, FTH$, FTHX and FTHS. Separate journals for each site's records, organized by UTC year and day.

Staged migration

The SQL-free journal service is available for the migration. Existing sites still use their current storage until their adapters and cross-ledger transactions are verified. No data reset has run.

Private by design

Ledger inspection and encrypted server-credential management live in Featheral Admin. This host does not expose private records, credential exports, arbitrary queries or public financial writes.
