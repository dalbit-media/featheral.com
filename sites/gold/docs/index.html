Off-chain first

Gold records a finite allocation from the origin wallet to the FTH$ backing wallet. This creates a pending obligation, not an Ethereum transfer. Dollar owns currency balances; World receives one-time market funding and players earn FTH$ by selling resources. Subsequent gameplay circulates existing currency through markets and treasury.

Reading reserve flows

Admin shows actual Ethereum holdings separately from OFF-SYNC incoming/outgoing commitments. Confirmed in/out counts are reconciled transfers since reserve initialization, not the wallets' lifetime history. Historical off-chain records remain off-chain records even after pooled obligations are settled. Separate receipt entries provide Ethereum evidence.

Conversion and manual settlement

Redemption requires confirmed, uncommitted backing and locks FTH$ in escrow. Confirmed returned FTHG can reconvert to unlock that same currency without new issuance. Only the two pinned reserve wallets participate. Initial allocation is automatic market funding, not a personal grant.

Mainnet sync is a separate, irreversible transfer.The conversion page only previews exact pending amounts and verifies existing finalized canonical FTHG receipts. It never sends Ethereum transfers. Off-chain signatures do not move tokens, and unsynchronized backing cannot redeem.
