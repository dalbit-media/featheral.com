Exchange and staking policy

    
    
      
  • Separate dynamic bridge contract; the existing FTHG contract remains unchanged and its fixed-rate bridge is legacy.
    • 
      
  • Opening spot rate is 1 FTHG : 1,000 FTH$. A constant-product curve changes quotes with funded pool balances; quotes fail closed until the bridge is deployed, real FTHG is deposited, and a matching FTH$ game-ledger budget is configured.
    • 
      
  • FTH$ is an off-chain game balance, not an on-chain token or a USD redemption promise. Each credit is posted only after its bridge event is confirmed and verified.
    • 
      
  • Staking locks FTHG principal. The initial base is 6% simple interest per game-year; the market spot quote is locked at stake time. Supported terms are 1, 2, 5, 10, 20, and 30 game-years. The signed game-service clock must be initialized on-chain before staking.
    • 
      
  • FTH$ staking rewards consume the configured game-ledger budget. They are credited only after a confirmed maturity claim; no server-held wallet keys are used.
    •
