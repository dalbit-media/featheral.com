Staking reward policy

FTHG principal is locked for 1, 2, 5, 10, 20, or 30 authoritative game-years, not calendar years. Simple interest uses the base APR and spot rate locked at stake creation. Rewards are reserved from the FTH$ budget then credited by the ledger only after a confirmed mature claim is indexed. Existing stakes keep their locked rewards.

Service and safety

Bridge safety

Pausing stops new conversions, redemptions, and stakes. Mature claims remain available.

Authoritative game clock

Only GAME_SERVICE_ROLE can initialize or advance the clock. Advancing the year can make stakes immediately claimable and cannot be reversed. Use the actual game-service year.

Authorize the game service

DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE is required. This role can advance the clock and submit redemptions; grant it only to the trusted settlement service.
