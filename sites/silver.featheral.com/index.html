From FTHS to redemption

  1. Convert FTH$ to FTHS in the inline wallet using funded Exchange pools, or use your existing FTHS.
  2. Choose an in-stock product and check its issuer, region, face value and redemption terms.
  3. Approve the exact FTHS payment in your wallet. One code is reserved; an administrator fulfills the order.
  4. Open My codes and reveal your delivered code. Redeem it with the listed issuer.

We do not ship physical goods. Silver and collectible listings must identify where and how their codes can be redeemed. Issuer delivery, fees and restrictions are stated per product.

Store terms

Undelivered paid orders can be refunded by Admin in FTHS to the original buyer, subject to the receiving wallet retaining the funds. Delivered codes need issuer review; this storefront cannot verify whether an external code has been redeemed.

About FTHS

Featheral Silver, also known as Featheral Salary, is a whole-unit native Notes currency used for marketplace redemption and approved Foundation contribution grants. It is not a silver weight claim, automatic wage, USD peg, or blanket promise of redemption. Only purchased listings carry their stated fulfillment terms.
